How it works

Matching. People are matched on email, projects on title. Records that already exist are updated; the rest are created. You can fix a typo and re-import the same file safely.

Passwords. New accounts are created with {{ default_password }} and are required to choose their own password the first time they sign in. Existing accounts keep the password they already have — re-importing never resets anyone.

All or nothing. If any row has a problem, nothing at all is saved and every problem is listed at once, so you fix them in a single pass.
